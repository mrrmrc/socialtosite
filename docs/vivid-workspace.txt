VIVID WORKSPACE — 29 October 2026

New visual direction
Indigo, violet, apricot and cyan replace the muted forest palette. Public
landing, login, dashboard, LIA, administration and the active editor are aligned.
SVG icons replace the navigation glyphs and appear in the project shortcuts,
SEO/GEO panels, profile menu and account controls. Administration typography
continues to use the shared scale checked in the previous correction.

Customer entry
The initial screen shows an observation panel before the LIA conversation:
Google impressions/clicks, informational GEO foundations, published content,
drafts, and links to activity, channels, statistics and services. The primary
navigation exposes LIA, content, website, SEO/GEO and channels.
No simulated charts, fabricated search scores or growth percentages are used.
Missing Google observations display a dash, not a false zero. GEO foundations
are local facts about identity, sources, content and contacts; the interface
explicitly says citations and presence in AI answers are not measured.

Personal account
User silhouette menu with name, plan, account editing, other settings and logout.
Supports keyboard focus, Escape and outside focus dismissal. Customer and admin
accounts can change their own name and login email. Email changes require the
current password; only authenticated account ID is used. Role, plan, slug and
other users' records cannot be changed through this endpoint. Name/email use
existing columns; there are no schema changes or migrations.
